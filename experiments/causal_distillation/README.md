# Output imitation and causal distillation

Qualified GRU output ensembles supplied scalar labels to students with selected observation schedules. In one fresh pair, the late-64 student reached 53/64 at t512 but 7/64 at t1024. Performance at an observed horizon was therefore not evidence of durable retention.

For the previously selected NCA, students were trained to imitate a known binary-only fold-repaired teacher. Derivatives of the known fold plus each **actual fixed learned update direction** predicted small fold shifts subsequently solved numerically. This is forward sensitivity conditioned on training, not unique mechanism reconstruction from outputs.
