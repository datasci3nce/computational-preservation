# Research checkpoint — 18 October 2026

**Status:** Frozen evidence snapshot, v1.0. No new experiments were run to prepare this checkpoint. Records existing executed studies and exploratory follow-ups. This is **not** a frozen publication submission, verified independent reproduction, or claim of a general interpretability/AI-safety method.

## The research question we keep

**What does it mean to preserve a learned computation when neural networks are aligned, merged, selectively modified, or distilled from outputs?** Can a causal/dynamical explanation predict which *future* behavior will survive, rather than only describe an observed endpoint?

We can now operationalize three distinct concepts:

1. **Observational equivalence:** equal task scores or equal responses over an observation set. Equal task *scores* are dramatically weaker than equal functions.
2. **Coordinate equivalence:** exactly function-preserving transformations (ANN/GRU hidden permutations; NCA global hidden-basis `O(2)`), provided the appropriate tensors transform consistently.
3. **Causal/dynamical equivalence:** matching responses to specified interventions and future continuations. Neither observational nor coordinate similarity automatically gives this for unrelated networks.

Different parameterizations can implement the same computation, and different computations can give the same score. Output imitation can be successful at one delay without preserving behavior at a later delay. **These are the conceptual conclusions**, not new theorems.

## Frozen findings: changes today

### A. Output-supervised GRU recomposition: time and identifiability

- Original three historically qualified GRUs (`61004`, `61067`, `61085`): scalar output ensemble had **100% strict three-way recall** and their aligned parameter average **0%** on the original 512-step 48-triplet bank. This was *same-task* merging, not complementary skill integration; the output average is **not** a full mixture of distributions or an LLM top-k procedure.
- A full student trained only on outputs through t64 had poor 512-step retention. Exploratory longer-horizon (t128/t256) teacher imitation improved to **89.6%/96.5%** standard noise on three new 48-triplet banks (144 total). This did not improve on simply retaining one competent parent.
- With exactly **64 teacher-labeled time points** and equal-length 256-step unroll, *early* supervision yielded **25.0%** strict 512 recall versus **100%** for *late* supervision in the selected trio. Late output constraints mattered beyond count of labels, but not all optimizer/data-budget effects were controlled and late-only was not uniformly best across other trios.
- Three additional disjoint trios of *previously saved* qualified parents had heterogeneous outcomes (easy even with early: 100%; late/dense rescue: up to 100%; and one with late 59.4% versus dense 99.0%). This is cross-trio evidence, not newly trained models.
- In a later **fresh-seed GRU cohort**, 6/24 newly trained networks passed qualification. A prechosen pair's aligned mean scored **0%** at t512, whereas late64 distillation scored **53/64 (82.81%)** at t512 but only **7/64 (10.94%)** at t1024. Dense256 scored 34/64 at 512 and 1/64 at 1024. Thus, neither t512 success nor short-sequence imitation established durable memory.
- A single current-output-aligned hidden-state correction to the *undistilled* damaged GRU failed at t512; a privileged teacher observation later allowed state assimilation to help an **already partly useful** distilled student. Neither is Bayesian state inference or standalone model discovery.

**Evidence:** `sources/OUTPUT_ASSUMPTIONS_MINI_V1.md`, `sources/CAUSAL_DISTILLATION_BATCH_20261018.md`, `sources/MODEL_MECHANISM_OOD_20261018.md`.

### B. NCA: observed outputs -> actual weight update -> tracked state-space dynamics

- The old **selected**, merge-derived seven-cell NCA seed-4/5 case has a known local saddle-node fold at `lambda*=0.20091324` on a *restricted F-to-S path between two merged variants*, **not** a path directly between both competent parents. Neither source parent pair was jointly qualified for robust strict three-way memory.
- From a previously successful **binary-only bias-patched version of that same model**, output-trajectory distillation gave new student parameter updates. On 48 matched t512 triplets (standard noise): readout-only **0/48**, input+bias **48/48**, recurrent coefficients **42/48**, full update **47/48**; the known teacher **46/48**. **All conditions 0/48 strict three-way** because no-cue behavior still fails. The teacher was known in advance and is not an independent trained NCA.
- With *each learned update direction held fixed*, a first-order fold-shift calculation was saved before the nonlinear fold solver: at 10% update, input+bias predicted 0.175609 (solved 0.175534), recurrence 0.189535 (solved 0.189469), full t128 0.183086 (solved 0.183100). Readout-only left the tracked fold unchanged. First-order accuracy is local; it does not prove full-parameter causal mediation.
- Intermediate-strength predictions were frozen before **new cue-bank** observations: behavioral binary recall rose gradually as tracked folds passed toward/through the t512 operating regime. A post-hoc bias-only edit calibrated to *match* the fold gave comparable recall on three of four selected comparisons, but **not exactly identical outcomes** on the recurrent-only path. Fold position is an informative **within-model coordinate**, not a sufficient state descriptor or proof of unique mediation.
- Exploratory fold-gap forecasting on held-out parameter-update *family*, still one NCA, beat an eta-only fraction predictor descriptively (group-fraction MSE **0.0293 vs 0.0387**). This is **not** an observation-only Murphy-style causal world model and not independent-model transfer.

**Evidence:** `sources/CAUSAL_DISTILLATION_BATCH_20261018.md`, original `paper/RESEARCH_NOTE.md` and `paper/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`.

### C. Synthetic failure-mode and model-OOD controls

- On qualified *newly trained* GRUs, **planted** readout-bias `+0.8`, input-zero, and recurrent-weight `×0.65` faults can all yield **0%** strict t512 recall while having different hidden information: readout fault leaves hidden trajectory unchanged; input fault erases cue encoding; recurrent fault initially succeeds at t64 but hidden separation decays by t512.
- A frozen prediction that a general 64-trained replacement head would restore **at least 90%** of the readout-fault cases **failed** on two held-out models (85.9%); that same head also degraded undamaged held-out models. A **post-result, restricted scalar bias calibration** rescued the planted bias corruption, but this was chosen knowing the fault family.
- Four new trained GRUs had output-null perturbations with negligible **immediate** output effect yet measurable **future** output effect. This does not identify the selected V6 candidate circuit.
- Three **hand-engineered**, non-trained clock/memory systems produced exactly identical early outputs but diverged later under passive continuation; clock intervention revealed different mechanisms. This is an *identifiability construction*, not learning an unknown mechanism.

**Evidence:** `sources/MODEL_MECHANISM_OOD_20261018.md`.

### D. Thesis-era ANN selective Homing pilot

- We used *the recovered `evo_test (2).zip` source*, with a headless Pillow simulation shim rather than native Pygame reproduction. Archived executable ANN uses eight raw sensory channels and **48 stored weights**, differing from the prose thesis's 7-input/36-weight model. Historical merged `.npy`, `.npz`, and source specialists do **not** reconcile into a single verified thesis Figure 7.1/7.7 checkpoint lineage.
- The archived **Homing specialist reached the goal on only 1/8 matched 600-step starts** (5/8 crashes); the saved LAP-labeled merge 0/8. Thus there is no reliably competent baseline in this archive for preserving Homing while selectively suppressing Exploration. The old thesis-reported 100%→0% composite pruning contrast is **not independently reproduced** from these files.
- On fixed observed sensor states, altering only the light input changed mean steering by **0.1577** for Homing specialist, **0.0374** for Exploration, **0.0210** for saved merge, and **0.00013** for saved refined ANN. This is a concrete light-to-output causal intervention, **not** successful Homing.
- Action distillation from the weak Homing specialist restored a full student's held-out action MSE to **0.00183**, and light-to-steering sensitivity to **0.1785**, but closed-loop goal success stayed **1/8** with **5/8 crashes**. No selective Exploration suppression was optimized or demonstrated.
- Removing the fully distilled model's hidden neuron 3 reduced fixed-sensor light-steering response **0.1785→0.0114**, while mean forward speed was largely unchanged; valid unit-specific causal contribution to light-responsive actions *in that chosen basis*, not a dedicated invariant Homing neuron. Exact consistent hidden-unit permutation preserves output numerically (~1e-16). The same 0/8 goal score across controllers does not mean their functions are equal.

**Evidence:** `sources/ANN_SELECTIVE_DISTILL_V1.md`; source/audit provenance embedded in the separate `ANN_SELECTIVE_DISTILL_V1.zip` experiment archive.

## What this changes in the previously frozen scientific account

**Keep the original NCA fold paper's scope unchanged:** established local fold/ghost and state/parameter interventions in **one selected NCA**. The new work can be a **versioned experimental addendum** on the effects of *actual output-distillation update vectors*. It does not make the paper a general merging or safety-preservation method.

**Keep GRU V6 separate:** it establishes conditional rescue of a signed effect by restoring one candidate-state contribution under a particular artificial clamp; not proof that ordinary distillation follows that same gate mechanism. Some fresh GRUs reproduce only the weaker output-null-future-influence phenomenon.

**Keep the thesis ANN pilot separate:** it exposed source provenance issues, weak navigation competence, and a useful light-to-steering intervention, not successful selective removal of Exploration.

**Do not unify parameter basins, state-space attractor basins, and same-score equivalence.** `O(2)` and permutation coordinate symmetries are established structure, not evidence of discovered physical symmetries or a unique circuit.
